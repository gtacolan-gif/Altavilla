Our property is open 24/7 to welcome you at any time of the day.

Every three days we change the bed linen and towels in all rooms, and every day we restock soap and toilet paper to always guarantee a comfortable stay.

Please remember that whenever you leave the property, it is mandatory to leave your keys at the reception.

We offer a rich breakfast, served from 8:00 AM to 10:00 AM.

If it is not included in your rate, the cost is €7.50 per person. For payment, please contact the reception.

  • Colosseum, Roman Forum & Palatine Hill 1.6 km · 20 min walk Guided tours, audioguides, or fast-track entry.
  • Trevi Fountain 1.4 km · 17 min walk Walking tours and underground tours available.
  • Pantheon 2 km · 25 min walk
  • Piazza Navona Underground 2.3 km · 28 min walk
  • Castel Sant'Angelo 3.2 km · 40 min walk
  • Vatican 4.5 km · 55 min walk (We recommend taking Metro Line A from Termini)
  • Capitoline Museums 2 km · 25 min walk
  • Borghese Gallery 2.5 km · 30 min walk
  • Hop-on Hop-off & Food Tours 0.3 km · 4 min walk

Distances are approximate. For schedules and availability, please ask at the reception.
